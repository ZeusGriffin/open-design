## Public code/tools identified

### CleanYourTV
Directly matches the TV example.
- Repo: https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv
- Purpose: reversible Android/Google TV debloat + minimal launcher
- Notable safety design: backups, package disabling instead of uninstalling, restore path, model-specific lists

### Final Fantasy Explorers / 3DS community servers
Matches the game-revival example.
- Repo: https://github.com/FreakyBigFoot/fbf-3ds-servers
- Purpose: self-hosted community servers for Final Fantasy Explorers / Fantasy Life on top of Pretendo
- Note: no game files are distributed; users must own their games

## Verification notes
- Many examples are third-party posts shown in the video, not demonstrations run by the narrator.
- Some reported numbers are anecdotes and should be independently verified before treating them as benchmarks.
- Scientific and medical examples are research stories, not proof of treatment effectiveness.
- Planet examples discussed candidates, not confirmed discoveries.
- The enzyme example was described as a newly surfaced candidate/system whose function was not yet established.
- Device changes should be matched to exact model/firmware and kept reversible.
