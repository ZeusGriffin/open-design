WUZPLAY CYBERDECK v9 — iPHONE SHORTCUTS SETUP

Phone-assisted actions run on the iPhone. Wuzplay does not have Internet access.

Create Apple Shortcuts with the exact names used by the NFC presets you import. Suggested names:

GOVEE
Each Govee Shortcut should perform the corresponding Govee action using the phone-side integration available to you.

FLASHLIGHT
The Flashlight Shortcut should toggle or turn on the iPhone flashlight. iOS permissions/automation behavior may require confirmation depending on the setup.

FIND MY CAR
Use a phone-side Shortcut that opens or navigates to the saved/parked-car location available on the phone. Behavior depends on the phone's permissions and available location data.

MEDITATION CYBER
Deploy the included Meditation_Cyber_Web/index.html to a public HTTPS URL. Encode that real URL in the Meditation Cyber NFC card. Do not use a fake URL.

IMPORTANT NFC LIMITATION
A Wuzplay button shortcut can select/activate an NFC preset, but NFC still requires the phone to be physically close enough to read the emulated tag. Arbitrary button-only remote triggering over Bluetooth requires a separately tested iOS BLE receiver path and is not claimed by this build.
